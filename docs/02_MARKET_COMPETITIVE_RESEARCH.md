# Market landscape and positioning — desk research
**Date:** 2026-10-19 | **Research method:** Public vendor product pages only; no customer interviews or paid product trials.

## Market context
Investment firms need accurate, synchronized financial records across internal systems and external counterparties. Reconciliation, data normalization, controls, and operational workflow are established enterprise product categories. This is NOT a claim that ReconIQ invented a new market.

## Named adjacent products
| Vendor / product | Publicly advertised scope | Implication for ReconIQ |
|---|---|---|
| Arcesium Opterra | Reconciliations and accounting, allocations, treasury, and other investment operations tools | Proof that recon is central to Arcesium's domain; focus the demo on transparent analyst triage, not pretending to replace Opterra. |
| Arcesium Aquata | Ingest, validate, harmonize and govern financial data; AI copilot and workflows | Position as a lightweight analyst-facing workflow layer adjacent to data integrity tools. |

## Differentiation hypothesis
The demo focuses tightly on **evidence-first exception triage**: comparison of internal and broker source values, readable next-check guidance, visible severity ordering, auditable changes, and workflow clarity. This is a demo positioning hypothesis, not a claim that existing vendors lack these capabilities.

## Segmentation hypothesis
- Beachhead: operations analysts at smaller investment managers and outsourced middle-office teams dealing with spreadsheet/manual reconciliations.
- Decision maker: head of investment operations.
- User: analyst, operations team lead.
- Buyer concerns: operational risk, auditability, integrations, security, implementation burden.

## Discovery research plan (NOT completed)
Interview 5–8 current or former buy-side operations analysts. Ask:
1. Walk me through your last exception investigation from start to resolution.
2. Which feeds generate the most manual follow-up?
3. What information do you copy between systems today?
4. What makes a break urgent or dangerous?
5. What evidence must be attached before a resolution can be approved?
6. Where do your current tools fail or excel?
7. Which metrics do team leads actually review?
8. What security / audit policies would a vendor need to meet?
Avoid leading questions like “Would AI make this better?” Instead observe workflows.
