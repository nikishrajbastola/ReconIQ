PORTFOLIO PROTOTYPE · 2026

From fragmented trade data to trusted operations.

ReconIQ is a portfolio demonstration of institutional financial reconciliation. It combines deterministic transaction matching, prioritized exception management, evidence-based investigation hypotheses and an auditable resolution workflow.

Audience

Investment operations analysts, team leads, operations product managers

Key design principle

Financial calculations remain deterministic and explainable

Guardrail

Analysts review changes; no automated ledger edits

Scope

Synthetic CSV-based data; no real brokerage integrations

Investigation guidance is rule-based in this fully offline demo. AI investigation is proposed in the roadmap, not represented as a completed capability.
